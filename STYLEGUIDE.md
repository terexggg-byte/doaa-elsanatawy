Style guide — دعاء السنطاوي

Purpose
- A minimal premium visual system for an Arabic luxury makeup brand.

Colors
- Primary gold: #C9A84B (var(--gold))
- Accent gold: #B88A2A (var(--gold-strong))
- Background: #F8F4F0 (var(--beige))
- Ivory: #FFFFFF (var(--ivory))

Typography
- Headings: `Cairo` (display), weights 600-800
- Body: `Tajawal`, weights 300-700
- Scale: h1 large display on desktop, h2 medium, h3/h4 for smaller sections

Spacing
- Tokens: --space-xxs .. --space-xl
- Generous vertical rhythm on sections: prefer `space-lg` / `space-xl` for hero and main sections.

Buttons
- Primary CTA: `gold-btn` — subtle gradient and lift on hover
- Secondary CTA: `cta-outline` — light border and calm tone

Imagery
- Large cinematic images with soft crop and rounded corners
- Use `image-frame` for subtle gold border and elevated shadow

Motion
- Small entrance reveals using `.reveal` with staggered delays
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

Accessibility
- Skip link present and visually exposed on focus
- Focus-visible outlines for keyboard users
- Clear aria labels on interactive elements (WhatsApp panel)

Components
- Navbar: minimal, brand left, nav links with subtle underline on hover
- Hero: cinematic background, short descriptive heading, clear CTAs
- Services/Portfolio: spaced cards with clear CTAs and high-quality imagery

Notes
- Maintain whitespace and restraint: remove decorative clutter.
- Keep copy concise and confident.
